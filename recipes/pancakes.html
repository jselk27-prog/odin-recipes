Pancakes

Description

Fluffy, golden pancakes that are perfect for a cozy breakfast. Easy to make and delicious for the whole family.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, whisk milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Combine wet and dry ingredients, mix until smooth.
  4. Heat a pan over medium heat and pour 1/4 cup batter per pancake.
  5. Cook until bubbles form, flip, and cook until golden brown.
  6. Serve hot with syrup or toppings of your choice.
