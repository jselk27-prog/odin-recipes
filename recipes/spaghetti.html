Spaghetti

Description

This classic spaghetti recipe features perfectly cooked pasta topped with a savory tomato sauce. Simple ingredients combine to create a meal that’s comforting and satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Fresh basil for garnish

Steps

  1. Boil water in a large pot and cook spaghetti according to package instructions.
  2. In a pan, heat olive oil and sauté garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add tomato sauce to the pan, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 5–10 minutes.
  4. Drain the spaghetti and combine with the sauce.
  5. Serve hot, garnished with fresh basil.
