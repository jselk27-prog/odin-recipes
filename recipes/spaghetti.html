Spaghetti

Description

Classic spaghetti with tomato sauce is an easy and comforting meal. It’s a family favorite and perfect for weeknight dinners.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef or Italian sausage
  • 1 jar (24 oz) tomato sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese for serving

Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti noodles according to package directions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Sauté onion and garlic until translucent.
  3. Add ground beef or sausage and cook until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. Stir in tomato sauce, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 10–15 minutes.
  5. Serve sauce over cooked spaghetti noodles.
  6. Top with grated Parmesan cheese and fresh herbs if desired.
